Dear Honey,

I don't think I say this enough, but I'm really grateful that you're in my life.

We've known each other for three and a half years, and somehow getting to know you has been one of my favourite stories to live. From Udaipur to garba nights, from our first kiss to all the completely ordinary moments in between, you've become such a big part of my life.

I love that you listen to all my rants even when I know I am being a little dramatic. I love that you tell me the truth instead of simply telling me what I want to hear. I love that you're a realist when I am very much in my feelings, and that somehow you balance me without ever making me feel like I need to be less myself.

And yes, you're ridiculously handsome. I will continue to complain about how unfair that is.

But more than anything, I love how it feels to spend time with you. Big adventures are amazing, but so are the tiny moments that nobody else would think twice about. Those are the moments I keep.

Happy National Boyfriend's Day, Honey. Here's to this chapter — and all the ridiculous, beautiful chapters still waiting for us.
